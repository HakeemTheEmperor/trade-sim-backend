# Adding a ticker is just adding a line. The nightly job profiles new symbols
# through FMP (FMP_DAILY_BUDGET a run, resuming the next night), then live
# prices and history pick them up automatically. Removing a line does NOT delete
# the stock, since users may hold it.
#
# Only list symbols Alpaca's IEX feed trades. OTC tickers and thinly traded
# ADRs may never receive a price; the staleness report will flag them.
#
# Dual-class tickers written with a dot (BRK.B, BF.B) are left out: FMP and
# Alpaca spell them differently (BRK-B vs BRK.B), so one provider would miss them.
